\textbf{Rfam.} Full FASTA matches to 5S rRNA (RF00001) and vault RNA (RF00006) contain 597,812 source records~\cite{rfam2025}. Global uniform reservoir sampling (seed 20261016) fixes 12,391 database, 20 exploratory and 50 confirmation records. Only FASTA headers and wrapped lines are parsed: no case/U--T normalization, character/length filtering, deduplication, family cap, cropping, mutation or augmentation is applied. The database retains 12,323 5S/68 vault records, 10,326 distinct contents and lengths 48--178; ambiguity symbols are literal bytes.

Confirmation identities are disjoint from database/exploratory identities. The sampled queries are all 5S, lengths 79--123; 11 match database content and remain included. The other 39 form a descriptive sensitivity check. IID uses shuffled round-robin ownership; Non-IID assigns each family to half the providers without rebalancing family volumes (four vault shards of 17 at $m=8$). These are simulated assignments. A separate fixed-seed concentration check (20261015, $m=8$, $\rho=0,.5,1$) mixes fraction $1-\rho$ and sends fraction $\rho$ to four home providers. All Rfam panels/tables use the same records and 50 queries: 40 native sweep/ablation configurations (23,400 runs), three concentration configurations (2,700), and two transport configurations (1,500).
